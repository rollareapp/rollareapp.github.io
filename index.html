What Rollare does

  • 8 tournament formatsSingle elimination, double elimination, round robin, and more — including Swiss and group + knockout, so you can run a real league day, not just a ladder.
  • Live score entry with an audit trailReport results from any device during the event. Every change is logged, so disputed rounds and corrections stay transparent.
  • Poster-grade PNG & PDF exportOne click turns your final standings and brackets into print-ready graphics for socials, notice boards and the venue wall.
  • Public tournament pagesShare a link and anyone can follow standings, fixtures and results in real time — no app install, no account needed to watch.

Built for the person holding the clipboard

Rollare is made for community organizers: game shops running weekly ladders, offices running lunch-hour esports, schools running club leagues, and tournament crews that outgrew chat-group scorekeeping. The flow is deliberately short — set up, seed, play, publish — because on event day nobody has time to learn software.

Everything runs in the browser. Players check in on their phones, the organizer enters or approves scores, and the public tournament page updates as rounds close. When it's done, export the standings as a poster and the event lives on beyond the venue.
